Approximation of a sum with an integral:
$$
\sum_{\bf m}\approx\int\frac{d^3pL^3}{(2\pi\hbar)^3}.
$$
Orthogonality relation of momentum states:
$$
\langle{\bf p}|{\bf p}'\rangle=
(2\pi\hbar)^3\delta^{(3)}({\bf p}-{\bf p}').
$$
Their completeness relation:
$$
\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi\hbar)^3}|{\bf p}\rangle\langle{\bf p}|=1.
$$
